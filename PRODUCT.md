# Paycebo

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
adaptive

## Stack
React Native with Expo, TypeScript, Expo Router, NativeWind, AsyncStorage.

## Users
Individual INR users who want a tangible, low-friction ritual for saving toward personal goals.

## Product Purpose
Make saving feel like paying a contact. The user reserves part of a manually entered bank balance for goals and records the reasons behind each contribution.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Local-only MVP: setup, separate demo data, goal editing, contribution and withdrawal chats, progress rings, weekly pledges, in-app reminders, activity, balance updates, and a tone preference. Money does not move. No bank integration, accounts, remote AI, cloud sync, or background notifications.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Paycebo. Notio visual reference, adapted to dark native screens, with payment-app interactions. Playful voice by default; supportive copy is always available. Use INR and Indian number formatting.

## Product Principles
- Bank balance is an input, not a verified account connection.
- Savings are allocations; never subtract contributions from the bank balance a second time.
- Never shame someone into keeping funds they need.
- Confirm changes only after local persistence succeeds.
- Clearly distinguish sample data from personal data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Scalable text, screen-reader labels, 48-unit controls, reduced-motion support, sufficient contrast, native back navigation, and keyboard-safe forms.
